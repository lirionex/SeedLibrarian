# Art and asset strategy

- **One procedural plant system** (L-systems from a genome) carries the entire flora budget. Infinite variety from one shader.
- **Plants need no skeletal animation** — vertex wind only. This is a big deal for a small team.
- **Tight palette + one soft toon-shading pass** gives coherence across everything.
- **Roughly fifteen hand-made meshes** could carry the whole game: house kit, benches, still, press, cistern, panel, windmill, wellhead, a few props.
- **Terrain and biomes procedural**; wild populations placed by rule rather than by hand.
- The notebook illustration is generated from the same genome that grows the plant — one system, two outputs.
