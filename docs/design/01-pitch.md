# Pitch

*A cozy 3D botany game about breeding plants, keeping a library, and slowly going self-sufficient.*

You run a small seed library at the foot of a valley. People bring you requests — some practical, some personal — and you cross plants across generations until you find something that fits. Everything worth keeping goes into your notebook, one page per specimen, permanently. Over time the notebook becomes your toolkit, the valley becomes your gene pool, and the garden outside your door becomes the thing you were really making all along.

Solo or 2–4 player co-op. Procedural flora. Soft music.
