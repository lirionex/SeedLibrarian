# Co-op (2–4)

Shared notebook, separate work. The natural roles:

- **Field** — expeditions, wild collection, mapping where traits live
- **Bench** — running crosses, managing beds, greenhouse conditions
- **Counter** — reading requests, negotiating, deciding what's worth committing to
- **Works** — processing, utilities, building

Nothing requires two players simultaneously (that turns into chore coordination). Instead, work compounds: someone isolating "thornless" last week silently makes your job easier today. A player can drop in, do one thing well for an hour, and have contributed.

## Implementation status

_Not started. Explicitly out of scope for the POC. Whether co-op shares one economy or only one library is an [open question](open-questions.md). Roles map onto the systems in [wild-valley](07-wild-valley.md), the bench/[core-loop](03-core-loop.md), [requests](06-requests-and-standing-orders.md), and [between-generations](08-between-generations.md)._
