# Design pillars

**No task list.** The world runs its own slow processes — plants grow, seasons turn, wild populations shift. The player's goals come from their own taste and from gaps in their own notebook, not from assignments.

**You can lose stock, but never knowledge.** Nothing the player has learned or catalogued can be destroyed. Setbacks cost time, never progress.

**Difficulty comes from trade-offs, not rarity.** Rare things run out. Antagonistic traits never do.

**Connected but never blocking.** In co-op, nobody's work requires anyone else's presence, but everyone's work compounds.

**Cheap to build, deep to play.** One procedural plant system carries the entire art budget.
