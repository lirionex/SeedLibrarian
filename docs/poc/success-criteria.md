# Success criteria

Behavioural, not opinions. Ask nobody whether they liked it.

**Strong signals:**
- A tester says "one more cross" and keeps playing past the point you told them to stop
- A tester forms a plan out loud *before* acting — "if I cross this with the ridge one, I should get…"
- A tester names a plant unprompted, or is annoyed when they lose one
- A tester ignores the request you gave them to pursue something they invented
- **Anyone asks "can I keep this save?"**

**Failure signals and what they mean:**

| Signal | Diagnosis |
|---|---|
| "What should I do now?" | Goals aren't legible. Standing order isn't visible enough, or the notebook's gaps aren't showing |
| Testers just spam random crosses | Genetics aren't legible. They can't form a plan, so they gamble. Add instruments or simplify the model |
| Boredom during growth | Not enough parallel state. Add beds or widen the stagger before adding *content* |
| "It's fine" with no engagement | The trade-offs aren't sharp enough. Traits aren't fighting each other hard enough to make choices interesting |

Five testers, twenty minutes each, watching silently. Note where they hesitate and what they say aloud.
