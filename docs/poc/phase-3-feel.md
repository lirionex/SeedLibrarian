# Phase 3 — Feel (only after both pass)

One plant, rendered properly. The procedural L-system, the toon shader, wind. Grow it in the greybox valley.

**You're checking:** does the procedural system produce plants that are actually *pretty*, and pretty in a **varied** way? This is the largest technical risk in the whole project — if procedural plants come out looking samey or ugly, the art strategy collapses and the budget assumptions with it.

Do this before committing to anything else visual.

## Gate

> Procedural plants are pretty and varied.
